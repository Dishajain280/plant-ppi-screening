plant-ppi-screening

A harness for screening plant protein–protein interactions with structure prediction: resolve the proteins, batch the jobs within a free quota, parse the confidence metrics, and score the ranking against known positives and honest negatives.

The problem

Finding an interaction by yeast two-hybrid or BiFC costs weeks per pair, so a transcription factor family cannot be screened that way. Structure prediction reverses the order of work: rank hundreds of pairs cheaply, then spend bench time on the top few percent. On plant proteins the naive version fails quietly — few structures are solved, alignments are thin, and disordered regions drag every confidence score down. A pipeline that submits full-length sequences and sorts by pLDDT looks confident and is wrong.

What the harness does

  1. resolve — gene symbols to UniProt entries, with a synonym fallback and explicit accession pinning for ambiguous symbols.
  2. check-labels — rejects duplicate pairs, placeholder sources, and a negative class that is mostly easy decoys.
  3. batch — jobs within the 30-per-day quota and the 5,000-token cap; over-budget pairs are skipped with a printed reason.
  4. ingest / evaluate — ipTM and chain-pair PAE into a ranked table, AUC with bootstrap intervals, calibration gap reported separately.
